Hermes Tasks

Hermes Tasks is a personal, single-user assistant tool. It lets the owner's own self-hosted AI assistant read and update the owner's Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides on the owner's behalf.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
