Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Hermes Tasks is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. No other person's data is accessed.

  • Data accessed: the owner's Google Calendar, Tasks, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides, only as needed to carry out the owner's requests.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens and any working data are stored on the owner's private, self-hosted server. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.
  • Processing: content may be sent to the AI model providers the owner has chosen, solely to answer the owner's own requests.
  • Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
